# PBR texture assets (CC0)

All textures are downloaded at build time by `.github/scripts/fetch-visual-assets.sh`
from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) and are released under **CC0 1.0** (public
domain, no attribution required; credited here anyway). 1k JPG diffuse/roughness and
OpenGL-convention PNG normal maps are used.

| Asset | Used for | Source |
|---|---|---|
| plastered_wall | whitewashed building walls | https://polyhaven.com/a/plastered_wall |
| cobblestone_pavement | town sidewalks (legacy streets) | https://polyhaven.com/a/cobblestone_pavement |
| precast_stone_paving | corniche promenade and terrace paving | https://polyhaven.com/a/precast_stone_paving |
| large_sandstone_blocks_01 | Borj el Kebir fortress, seawall | https://polyhaven.com/a/large_sandstone_blocks_01 |
| beige_wall_001 | balustrade, curbs, planters | https://polyhaven.com/a/beige_wall_001 |
| coast_land_rocks_01 | coastal rocks, fortress point, palm bark | https://polyhaven.com/a/coast_land_rocks_01 |
| asphalt_02 | corniche road | https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02 |
